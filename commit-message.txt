Rebuild the visual layer: a fixed instrument panel

Three faults, all fair, and the third one named the direction the first two were
looking for.

Not responsive. There is now no viewport media query anywhere in the stylesheet,
and layout.spec.ts asserts that by reading the CSS source rather than by hoping. The
window opens fullscreen with a 1280x800 minimum. A professional panel does not
rearrange itself: an operator's hand knows where the In field is.

Not scrollable. The frame is a grid exactly the height of the window and nothing in
it overflows. Three zones scroll inside themselves - the queue, the transcript hits
and the log - because those are the only ones that can hold an unbounded number of
rows, and an unbounded list gets its own well rather than moving the application
around it.

The palette and the type were the real problem, and the fix was to stop designing a
dashboard. The stance is now *tactical telemetry*: a rack panel. Ten skills went into
it - industrial-brutalist-ui for the substrate and the one-accent rule,
typography-and-spacing-scale for a Major Third from a 12px base, dark-mode-color-
systems for semantic tokens and reduced eye strain, design-tokens-to-css for the
mapping, high-end-visual-design, ui-ux-pro-max, frontend-design, accessibility,
web-design-guidelines and ux-feedback.

  - Substrate: off-black in six even steps. Not #000, which makes a video frame look
    washed out and every hairline invisible.
  - One accent: hazard red, for the focus ring, the selected row's edge and a
    failure. Nothing else.
  - One signal: terminal green, on exactly one element - the ffmpeg lamp. A single
    instrument that reads as live. Anywhere else it would stop meaning anything.
  - Type: Cascadia Mono as the *dominant* face, because a column of timecodes has to
    line up digit for digit and that is the job. Segoe UI Variable for prose only.
    Both already on the machine: a desktop app that downloads a font is broken for
    the first half-second and unusable offline.
  - Geometry: 90 degrees everywhere. Not one border-radius in the file.
  - Zones: a 22px header strip with a 9px caps title, a rule, a body. Separation is a
    hairline and a change of substrate, not a card floating on a background.
  - A 26px row.

The zones are Video, Caption file, Range, Options, Queue, Find in the transcript and
Progress - the compartments a broadcast panel would have, with the queue, the search
and the log as compartments of their own rather than built around the form. The plan
line under the marks is monospace and boxed between rules, so "lossless copy" reads
as an instrument readout rather than as a sentence.

Also corrected: the declared window minimum was 1000x620 from the previous layout; it
is 1280x800, which is what the panel is designed for and tested at.

The engine is untouched. crates/ is unchanged, and the head patch is re-verified end
to end: head encode 37..50, body copy 50.., join, all 9 forensic checks passing, body
byte-identical to the source by framemd5.
